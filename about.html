Our process

Welcome to Break Makers!

Break Makers manufactures innovative football card repacks through a careful, hands-on process—starting with intentionally sourced cards that are selected with quality and variety in mind.

How it’s made

Every product goes through a consistent workflow: obtaining cards, examining condition, organizing pack-out formats, packaging with clean presentation, sealing for integrity, and then preparing shipments so customers receive a premium, stream-ready experience.

Whether you’re breaking live on platforms like Whatnot or Fanatics Live, or you’re simply looking for a fun, exciting way to grow your personal collection, Break Makers repacks are designed to deliver a smooth unboxing and an enjoyable chase.
